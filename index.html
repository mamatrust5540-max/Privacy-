Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QR & Barcode Scanner ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information.

Privacy Commitment: We do NOT sell, rent, or collect your personal scans or personal data on remote servers. All scanned QR/Barcodes, notes, and generated codes remain strictly on your local device.

1. Permissions & Data Collection

  • Camera Permission: Required strictly for real-time scanning of QR codes and barcodes. Video feeds are processed entirely in real-time on your device and are never recorded or transmitted.
  • Storage / Media: Required only when you choose to scan a barcode from an existing photo or save a generated QR image/PDF to your device.
  • Vibration: Used to provide haptic feedback when a scan is successful.
  • Internet: Required to fetch web previews, open URLs when requested by you, and load third-party ad services.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to support app development. Google AdMob may collect and use advertising identifiers and anonymized diagnostic data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Data Retention & Security

All your scanning history, favorites, and generated codes are stored locally on your device using a private SQLite database. You can delete your history at any time inside the app settings.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
